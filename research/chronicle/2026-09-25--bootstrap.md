## Decisions

1. **Independent research identity.** The project is not named after a vendor. Hypermind or any other platform is an instrument candidate, not the scientific object.
2. **Shadow mode first.** Study 0 observes outcomes of activities that would occur anyway and keeps collective outputs away from operational decision owners before resolution.
3. **Forecast Contract as local formal object.** A question is admissible only when outcome semantics, time bounds, authoritative resolution evidence, resolver, exclusions and safeguards are prospective and recoverable.
4. **Private forecast before market exposure.** Independent forecasts are frozen before participants see the market aggregate so simple aggregation remains a real baseline.
5. **Primary unit is the resolved contract.** Trades and repeated timestamps are nested data, not independent experimental units.
6. **Public/private boundary.** Public artifacts use generic examples only. Any internal deployment overlay must live in an approved private store and pin this protocol immutably.
7. **No forced coupling.** Probabilistic ML, ESC/evidence contracts, human-machine ensembles and GO-ED-POMDP are future compatibility points. None is required to justify Study 0.
8. **Pedagogy is a deliverable.** Reusable notions are to be capitalized in Diderot under its own review flow; explanatory synthesis does not authorize scientific claims.
