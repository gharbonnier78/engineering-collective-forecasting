# Source note

## Supports

Prelec introduces a mechanism that combines a respondent's own answer with predictions about how answers are distributed in the population, rewarding answers that are more common than collectively predicted. It is a foundational precedent for eliciting meta-beliefs alongside first-order judgments.

## Does not support

The Bayesian Truth Serum addresses truthful elicitation of subjective data, not the specific binary engineering-forecast protocol used here. It does not establish that the project's meta-belief recalibration is an implementation of BTS or inherits its incentive properties.
