# Source note

## Supports
Google's internal prediction-market evidence documents optimism and correlated trading associated with organizational and social proximity. It is relevant to the risk that people forecasting outcomes they influence, or people sharing information networks, are not independent sensors.

## Does not support
It does not establish the magnitude or direction of bias in this engineering context and is not evidence that a market should be deployed in Study 0.
