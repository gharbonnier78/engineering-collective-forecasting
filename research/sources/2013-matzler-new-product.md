# Source note

## Supports

Prediction markets have been evaluated for new-product sales forecasting using consumer communities, providing precedent for product-oriented outcomes beyond financial or political markets.

## Does not support

Consumer sales forecasting is not equivalent to integration, qualification or release-readiness forecasting; no transfer claim is made.
