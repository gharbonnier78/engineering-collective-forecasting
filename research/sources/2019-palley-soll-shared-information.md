# Source note

## Supports
When judgments share information, asking respondents for both their own judgment and their estimate of others' judgments can help separate shared from private information and improve aggregation. This motivates collecting meta-predictions.

## Does not support
The project's simple logit meta-recalibration is not claimed to be Palley and Soll's exact pivoting algorithm.
