# Source note

## Supports
The studies report systematic differences between predictions about one's own completion times and observer predictions, motivating actor/observer status as a design factor rather than optional metadata.

## Does not support
The effect size from task-completion experiments is not assumed to transfer numerically to engineering forecast probabilities.
