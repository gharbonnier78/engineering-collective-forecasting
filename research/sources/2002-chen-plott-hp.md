# Source note

## Supports

A designed market-like information aggregation mechanism was deployed inside Hewlett-Packard for sales forecasting and the reported results compared favorably with the company's traditional forecasts in that setting. It is direct precedent for studying dispersed internal knowledge through structured forecasting mechanisms.

## Does not support

It does not establish that the same mechanism will outperform engineering experts, tests, or simple aggregation in a software qualification/release context.
