# Source note

## Supports

Corporate prediction markets can overweight public information under modeled hidden-profile conditions, and social information structure can matter. This motivates collecting independent pre-market forecasts and describing whether information is common or local to a role.

## Does not support

The model does not let this project infer private information content directly from market prices or prove a causal benefit of role diversity.
