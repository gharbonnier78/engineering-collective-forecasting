# Source note

## Supports

Market scoring rules combine individual probability elicitation with group information aggregation; the logarithmic form provides a canonical automated market-maker construction and useful conditional-probability properties.

## Does not support

LMSR is not assumed to be the only valid market mechanism and the present Study 0 does not claim its theoretical properties eliminate organizational biases, thin participation, or poor question design.
