# Source note

## Supports

The Surprisingly Popular principle uses a second-order prediction of population responses to recover minority knowledge that may be more informed than the majority response. It motivates treating meta-predictions as information about shared versus private beliefs rather than relying only on popularity.

## Does not support

The Nature method targets single-question crowd-wisdom problems and is not the same algorithm as the project's probability-forecast recalibration. Its empirical performance does not transfer automatically to engineering forecasts.
