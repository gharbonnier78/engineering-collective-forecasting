# Source note

## Supports
Directly elicited probability judgments can contain substantial forecasting information and provide a serious baseline for prediction markets. This supports the minimum-sufficient-mechanism redesign of Study 0.

## Does not support
It does not imply that private polls always dominate markets or that the same ranking holds for engineering events.
