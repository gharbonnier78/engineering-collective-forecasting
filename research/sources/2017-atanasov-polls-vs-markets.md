# Source note

## Supports

Prediction markets should be compared with strong non-market aggregation baselines. The published study shows that simple and enhanced polling/aggregation can be highly competitive with markets, making a market-only evaluation scientifically insufficient.

## Does not support

The results do not imply that polling is universally superior; mechanism performance depends on design, participants and context.
