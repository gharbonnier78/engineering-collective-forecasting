# Source note

## Supports

Proper scoring rules provide principled evaluation of probabilistic forecasts and encourage truthful probability reporting in expectation under their assumptions. The Brier/quadratic and logarithmic scores belong to this evaluation family.

## Does not support

A proper score does not by itself establish that a forecasting process is calibrated, unbiased, causal, or operationally useful.
