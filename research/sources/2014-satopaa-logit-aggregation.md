# Source note

## Supports

Satopää et al. derive and evaluate a parsimonious logit-scale probability aggregator. The work provides an external foundation for the broader observation that linear averaging can be too conservative and that calibrated extremization can improve aggregate probability forecasts in some settings.

## Does not support

The fitted geopolitical-forecasting results do not determine the correct extremization factor for engineering forecasting. The Study 0 factor remains prospectively fixed and must not be tuned on Study 0 outcomes.
