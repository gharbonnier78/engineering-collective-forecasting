# Source note

## Supports

Decision markets illustrate the additional incentive problems that appear when forecasts are conditioned on actions and then used to choose actions. The work is relevant to later decision-support studies.

## Does not support

Study 0 does not use conditional markets to estimate causal effects and does not let market probabilities choose engineering actions.
