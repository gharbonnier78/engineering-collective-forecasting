# Source note

## Supports

Across 116 published tests, Wood et al. report a small positive average effect of asking intention or self-prediction questions on subsequent behavior. This is relevant as external evidence that measurement can be reactive and therefore supports treating elicitation-induced reflection as a validity threat rather than assuming observation is behaviorally inert.

## Does not support

The effect size is not transferred to engineering teams. The domains, interventions and outcomes differ materially. In this protocol the literature motivates detection, reporting and sensitivity analysis for interference; it does not quantify engineering interference.
