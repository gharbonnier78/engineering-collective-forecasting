# Source note

## Supports

The study analyzes corporate prediction markets at Google, Ford and another firm under thinness, weak incentives and possible bias. It reports relatively efficient markets in those settings and improvement over expert forecasts at all three firms, with reductions in mean-squared error as large as 25%, while also documenting optimism bias at Google.

## Does not support

Those effect sizes are not transportable priors for this engineering context. Study 0 must estimate its own paired performance and preserve possible optimism or organizational bias as a threat.
