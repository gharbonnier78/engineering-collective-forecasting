# Source note

## Supports

Human and computational forecasts can be complementary when they rely on different signals and their errors are not perfectly aligned. This provides theoretical motivation for a later human-machine extension.

## Does not support

It does not establish that a specific engineering ML model plus a prediction market will improve accuracy. Study 0 keeps this as future work.
