# Source note

## Supports
Simple averaging of individual probabilities can be conservative. The paper distinguishes probability-scale compression and incomplete pooling of information as reasons an aggregate may benefit from extremizing.

## Does not support
No fixed extremization parameter is universally optimal. Study 0 therefore freezes any transform prospectively and does not tune it on its outcomes.
