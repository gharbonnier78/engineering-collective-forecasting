# Source note

## Supports
Meta-beliefs can be used to estimate a shared prior and recalibrate aggregate probability forecasts without requiring a long history of resolved questions. This is particularly relevant when a naive fixed prior may itself be biased.

## Does not support
The repository does not claim that its simplified fixed-a implementation reproduces the published algorithm or inherits its empirical performance.
