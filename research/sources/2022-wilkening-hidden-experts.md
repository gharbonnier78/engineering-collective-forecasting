# Source note

## Supports

Wilkening, Martinie and Howe use meta-predictions to identify and weight more informed forecasters in single-question problems without relying on historical individual track records. This strengthens the methodological case for collecting a peer-forecast meta-prediction as a separate variable.

## Does not support

The paper's algorithm and experiments are not the project's simplified fixed-a logit recalibration. Study 0 does not claim to reproduce the published algorithm or its reported performance.
