## Current scientific boundary

The independent review of the bootstrap design was PARTIAL ACCEPT. G1 remains closed. Study 0 was redesigned around the minimum-sufficient mechanism.

- Study 0A is a dry Forecast Contract funnel. It uses no participants and no market.
- Study 0B uses only private probability elicitation. No aggregate is exposed before resolution.
- Prediction markets are a later mechanism study, not the Study 0 primary intervention.
- Study 0 contains no empirical result until prospective data are collected.

The primary scientific unit is the resolved Forecast Contract, with prospective cluster_id for dependence. Participant responses are nested observations.

## Frozen-before-outcome rule

Before any Study 0B outcome is inspected, freeze at minimum:

- Forecast Contract, cluster and version/hash;
- event-family target population and base-rate construction rule;
- accountable-owner private forecast protocol and official-status capture;
- participant eligibility and actor/observer quotas;
- private forecast and meta-prediction fields;
- AI-assistant-consultation metadata;
- primary crowd aggregation rule and all constants;
- estimands, admissibility thresholds and scoring convention;
- stopping rule and continuation/non-inferiority margin where used;
- random seed and cluster-resampling plan.

No parameter may be tuned on Study 0 outcomes and then reported as preregistered.

## Non-interference rule

Study 0B is private shadow forecasting. Before resolution, collective aggregates are hidden from:

- participants;
- the accountable decision owner;
- operational governance;
- the principal investigator or study-team member whenever that person has decision authority within the contract's scope.

An independent data custodian controls pre-resolution aggregate access. Private elicitation itself may still trigger reflection; detected interference is recorded. Primary analysis retains all otherwise resolvable contracts (intention-to-observe); an interference-excluding analysis is sensitivity only.

## Institutional-reference rule

Incremental usefulness must be evaluated against what the organization already knows. At the same freeze time, record:

- historical base rate for the preregistered event family;
- accountable owner's private probability;
- official status already in force.

The official status is descriptive unless a numeric mapping was prospectively defined.

## Actor/observer rule

ability_to_influence is a design factor, not optional metadata. Predeclare minimum actor/observer participation or mark the contract outside the primary comparison. Report actor and observer aggregates separately.

## Confidentiality and HR rule

This public repository must not contain confidential operational data or live vendor evaluations. Individual forecasts, abstentions, meta-predictions, AI-use flags, scores and longitudinal track records must never be used for HR appraisal, ranking, compensation, discipline or promotion.

## Pedagogy and Diderot

Apply the Pedagogical Concept Contract. Reusable teaching notions belong in Diderot after source/provenance review. Current additions include collective forecasting, proper scoring/Brier convention, meta-prediction, shared-information correction, the Brier diversity identity and the project-local Forecast Contract.

## Mutation and review

- Keep PRs in draft until exact-head review is complete.
- Tests must fail under scientifically material mutations, especially event-level resampling substituted for cluster-level resampling.
- Preserve null, negative and ambiguity evidence.
- Independent reviewers do not edit, rebase, push or merge during review.
